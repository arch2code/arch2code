Wait, I don't understand. Re-pitch that: give me a little bit of context, talk in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, and use the ubiquitous language from the `CONTEXT.md` beside this file. Stop using jargon and speak coherently. State it more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another. Unless we are working on the plan, avoid plan references that I will need to go read to figure out what you are talking about.
